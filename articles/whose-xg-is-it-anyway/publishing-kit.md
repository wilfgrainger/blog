## Preview / social description
McBaggio looks like a find at 0.64 xG per 90. Mark asks whether the agent removed four penalties. It didn't: the rate the club actually asked for is 0.53, below its 0.55 cut-off. The tricky part of a business rule isn't the threshold. It's agreeing what the number means and which evidence produced it.
